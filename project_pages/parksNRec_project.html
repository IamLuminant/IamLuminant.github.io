Parks n Rec

Core Features

Visitor Management

Keep your visitors happy with:

  • Visitor satisfaction tracking
  • Feedback and review system
  • Special events and attractions
  • Accessibility options

Conservation

Balance nature and tourism:

  • Wildlife population management
  • Habitat preservation
  • Environmental impact tracking
  • Education programs

Budget Management

Run a sustainable park:

  • Income and expense tracking
  • Grant applications
  • Staff hiring and management
  • Facility upgrades

Dynamic Events

Face unexpected challenges:

  • Weather events
  • Seasonal changes
  • Animal encounters
  • Special visitor requests

Game Systems

Park Layout & Design

Design your park with trails, picnic areas, campsites, visitor centers, and more. Each element affects visitor satisfaction and environmental impact.

Wildlife Ecosystem

A simulated ecosystem where animal populations interact with each other and the environment. Your decisions affect the balance of nature.

Staff Management

Hire rangers, guides, maintenance workers, and administrative staff. Each has unique skills and needs that affect park operations.

Research & Education

Conduct research to unlock new features, improve conservation efforts, and create educational programs for visitors.
