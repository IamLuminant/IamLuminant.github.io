Design Philosophy

Capturing the Ghibli Spirit

The goal of this project is to capture the essence of Studio Ghibli films in an interactive medium. Key inspirations include:

  • My Neighbor Totoro - The wonder of discovering magical creatures in everyday places
  • Spirited Away - A richly detailed world filled with spirits and wonder
  • Princess Mononoke - The relationship between humanity and nature
  • Kiki's Delivery Service - Coming of age and finding your place in the world

The game emphasizes environmental storytelling, memorable characters, and moments of quiet beauty that allow players to simply exist in the world.
