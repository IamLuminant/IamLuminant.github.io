Just a Normal Gardening Game

A game jam project with one other person over the span of two days.

Game Jam Survival Singleplayer 3D
Play on Itch.io

Game Screenshot

Gameplay

In the game, you play as a gardener who has to grow plants and keep them alive, but during the night monsters come out and try to destroy you and your plants.

This project showcased our ability to work under tight deadlines while creating an engaging gameplay experience. The core loop involves:

  • Day Phase - Plant seeds, water plants, and prepare defenses
  • Night Phase - Defend your garden from monster attacks
  • Progression - Unlock new plants and tools as you survive longer

Game Jam Experience

This game was created in just two days as part of a game jam with one other developer. Key takeaways:

  • Rapid Prototyping - Quickly implementing core mechanics
  • Scope Management - Keeping features realistic for the timeframe
  • Collaboration - Effective communication and task division
  • Polish Under Pressure - Making the most of limited time
