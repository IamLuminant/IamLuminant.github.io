A narrative-driven game based on my original story and music album.

Indigo

The Story

A tale of identity, purpose, and redemption.

Indigo follows the journey of Ko, an escaped slave. In a world where magic and STEM run the world, Ko must find peace and freedom at a utopia while bringing on a team of unpopular people.

The story explores themes of redemption, identity, perseverance, and kindness. As players progress, they'll get accustomed to the harsh, violent world until they find peace and happiness within their friends.

The narrative is deeply intertwined with the music, with key story moments accompanied by original compositions from the Indigo album.

The Album

Indigo - Original Soundtrack

The Indigo album serves as both the soundtrack and the inspiration for the game. Each track corresponds to a key story moment or character theme.

Featured tracks include:

  • Kuroba - The theme of Rain
  • The Spectacle - An epic reprise of the theme Savoy
  • Red Sun - An epic reprise of the theme Rouge
  • Scarlet Flames - The theme of Rouge
  • Indigo - Theme of Ko
